About

Audio representations and generative models, from problem formulation to evaluation.

I am Byeonggeun Kim (김병근), a Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon AGI. My current work is on fully causal neural codecs for full-duplex speech-to-speech; before that, I led audio encoder pretraining and audio/music generation work at Amazon. Earlier, at Qualcomm AI Research, I worked on efficient architectures, few-shot and open-set recognition, transfer learning, and domain adaptation.
