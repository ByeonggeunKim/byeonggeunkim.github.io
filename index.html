Generative audio ICML 2025

Streaming audio generation with continuous-valued tokens

Continuous-valued audio tokens avoid the fidelity loss of discrete tokens, but are hard to generate causally, token by token, as streaming requires. I proposed masked next-token prediction (MNTP), a training task that brings continuous-audio fidelity to causal, token-by-token generation.

Result
AudioCaps FAD 33% lower than AudioGen Large.
Role
Proposed MNTP; primary research mentor.
Paper
02

Audio representations ICASSP 2025 · Oral

Single-stage audio encoder pretraining at scale

Leading self-supervised audio encoders such as BEATs rely on iterative pretraining, which is costly to scale. I led the design of a single-stage alternative with curriculum masking and scaled it to 400K hours of audio.

Result
Outperformed a reproduced BEATs baseline on all five frozen-encoder probes at matched model and data scale.
Role
Co-first author; led design and training.
Paper
03

Efficient neural architectures INTERSPEECH 2021

BC-ResNet: broadcasted residual learning

On-device keyword spotting needs tiny models, yet cheap 1D temporal convolutions lose the frequency structure that 2D convolutions keep at a much higher cost. I co-developed broadcasted residual learning, which keeps most computation temporal, and proposed its auxiliary 2D residual path, which preserves frequency information.

Result
BC-ResNet-1 matched TENet12's Speech Commands v1 accuracy with 10.9× fewer parameters and comparable compute.
Role
Co-first author.
Paper Code
04

Domain generalization DCASE 2021 · Task 1A

DCASE 2021 Challenge Winner

Task 1A asked for low-complexity acoustic scene classification across multiple recording devices. Looking past model compression, I identified frequency-dependent device shift as the key barrier and proposed Residual Normalization, which reduces device effects while preserving scene cues.

Result
1st place; log loss 0.724 vs. 0.758 for the runner-up team.
Role
First author of Qualcomm's winning submission.
